CUSTOMER SUPPORT • COMPLEX ENQUIRY
01

Untangling a Multi-Issue Account Request

Challenge

A customer was unable to contact support directly, so a representative contacted the team about an account involving an in-transit order, a refund request and account closure. Several processes depended on one another.

Approach

I completed the required verification before discussing the account, reviewed the order and account status, then explained the correct sequence: allow the shipment to return, process the refund, settle any remaining account balance and only then proceed with account closure.

Outcome

The customer received a clear resolution path and realistic expectations instead of conflicting promises. The case could move forward while remaining within the appropriate account, refund and closure process.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT • INVESTIGATION
02

Tracing a Historical Payment Discrepancy

Challenge

A customer disputed an outstanding balance and asked support to trace a previous payment. The relevant balance did not originate from the period the customer expected.

Approach

I reviewed historical account records across different transaction periods, traced the source of the outstanding amount and broke down the balance, including a small remaining amount the customer questioned.

Outcome

Once the records and calculation were explained clearly, the customer accepted the explanation and proceeded with payment, preventing the account from progressing further through the collections process.
